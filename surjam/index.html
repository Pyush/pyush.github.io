SurJam

Play 15 Indian & Western instruments offline, take lessons, tune and record jams.

Scan to installScan with your phone's camera to open Google Play.
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

Indian instruments

  • Tabla with 5 taals and a step sequencer
  • Dholak & Dhol: Garba, Bhangra and Bhajan beats
  • Harmonium with Sa/Pa drone keys
  • Sitar with meend, chikari and tarab strings
  • Bansuri with half-holes for komal swaras
  • Santoor in all 10 thaats, with tremolo rolls
  • Shehnai with meend, gamak and sur drone
  • Veena with gamaka pulls and tala strings

Western instruments

  • Multitouch piano with scales and chords
  • Guitar with 45+ chords and auto strum
  • Ukulele, violin and a rainbow xylophone
  • 16-pad drum machine: Classic, Hip-Hop, EDM
  • DJ loop launcher with BPM sync and filter FX

Learn and practise

  • Step-by-step lessons for piano, guitar and tabla
  • Ear training and a raga guide
  • Daily goal, practice streaks and progress charts
  • Chromatic tuner with a vocal pitch graph
  • English or Sargam (Sa Re Ga) note labels

Record and share

  • Record jams on any instrument
  • Replay them in the app
  • Share them as audio files

Privacy

No account and no sign-up. Your recordings and progress stay on your phone, and the tuner analyses sound on your device without recording it. SurJam is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first. Read the privacy policy.
