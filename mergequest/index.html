Merge Quest: Discovery Puzzle

A relaxing merge puzzle in a magical fantasy world. Drag matching items together to create rarer ones, fill orders for the grove spirits, marsh folk and gnomes, and restore a fading world chapter by chapter.

Coming soon to Google Play

What's inside

Merge and discover

  • Drag two matching items together to make a higher-tier one
  • 64 items across 8 families, from Aquatic Crystals to Enchanted Eggs
  • A Discovery Encyclopedia with the lore behind every find
  • Catalysts for special reactions and surprise discoveries

Restore a world

  • Fill orders from the patrons who visit your board
  • Every finished chapter restores a piece of the world
  • Travel through groves, marshes, caverns and more on the journey map

Always something new

  • A weekly event every Monday, with its own reward track
  • Daily rewards with a 7-day streak, and daily quests
  • Level up to unlock new biomes, bigger 8×8 and 9×9 boards and timed rifts
  • Ascend at the top for powerful astral perks

Play your way

  • No time limits on the main board
  • Undo, hints and auto-merge when you're stuck
  • Plays offline, with no account to create
  • In English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi and Indonesian

Free, with optional extras

Merge Quest is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases such as gem packs, the weekly Event Pass and the Piggy Bank. Ads are a banner on menu screens and an occasional ad at natural breaks, from level 3. Optional reward videos give bonuses without paying. Remove Ads, a one-time purchase through Google Play, turns off the banner and the in-between ads.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Your progress is stored on your phone. The internet is used for Google Play purchases and for ads from Google AdMob, which asks for consent where the law requires it. Read the privacy policy and terms.
