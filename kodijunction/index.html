Kodi Junction

The classic cowrie board game from Gujarat. Throw four shells, race your gotis around the board and bring them all home, against the computer, with family on one phone, or online with friends.

Scan to installScan with your phone's camera to open Google Play.
Get it on Google Play Visit kodijunction.com

What's inside

How to play

  • Throw four cowrie shells and count the open ones to move 1 to 4, or 8 when all land closed
  • Land on a rival goti to capture it and open the inner track
  • Stay safe on the X squares, and pair two gotis into a Jodko
  • First to bring all four gotis to the centre wins. Full rules

Game modes

  • Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard
  • Pass and play with 2, 3 or 4 players on one phone
  • Play online with 2, 3 or 4 players: invite friends with a link or match with anyone
  • Offline modes need no internet and no account
  • Hints show which gotis can move, and can be turned off

Collect and customise

  • Win coins in every match
  • 50 boards, 50 goti sets and 50 cowrie shell styles in the Heritage Bazaar
  • Preview any item before you unlock it
  • A free daily spin for bonus coins

For the whole family

  • Simple rules for kids and grandparents, with real strategy
  • English, Gujarati, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic and Japanese
  • Sound, vibration and fast computer moves can each be switched on or off

Free and Pro Pass

Kodi Junction is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Ads never appear during a match, and video ads play only when you choose to watch one for coins. The Heritage Pro Pass, a one-time purchase through Google Play, removes banner and between-match ads and adds 2,000 coins, the Diwali Festival board and a Pro badge. Coin packs are also available.

Kodi Junction: questions and answers

How do you play Kodi Junction?

Throw four cowrie shells and count the open ones to move 1 to 4, or 8 when all land closed. Capture rival gotis, stay safe on the X squares, and be first to bring all four gotis to the centre.

Can I play Kodi Junction online with friends?

Yes. Play online with 2, 3 or 4 players: invite friends with a link or match with anyone. You can also play the computer or pass and play on one phone.

Does Kodi Junction work offline?

Yes. Playing the computer and pass and play need no internet and no account.

Is Kodi Junction free?

Yes. Kodi Junction is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Ads never appear during a match, and the one-time Heritage Pro Pass removes banner and between-match ads.

Privacy

No account to create. Your coins, unlocked items and settings are stored on your phone. The internet is used for online matches (your player name and match moves), Google Play purchases, and ads from Google AdMob, which asks for consent where the law requires it. Read the privacy policy and terms.
