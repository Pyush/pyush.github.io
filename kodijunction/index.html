Kodi Junction

The classic cowrie board game from Gujarat. Throw four shells, race your gotis around the board and bring them all home, against the computer or with family on one phone.

Coming soon to Google Play

What's inside

How to play

  • Throw four cowrie shells and count the open ones to move 1 to 4, or 8 when all land closed
  • Land on a rival goti to capture it and open the inner track
  • Stay safe on the X squares, and pair two gotis into a Jodko
  • First to bring all four gotis to the centre wins

Game modes

  • Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard
  • Pass and play with 2, 3 or 4 players on one phone
  • Plays offline, with no account to create
  • Hints show which gotis can move, and can be turned off

Collect and customise

  • Win coins in every match
  • 50 boards, 50 goti sets and 50 cowrie shell styles in the Heritage Bazaar
  • Preview any item before you unlock it
  • A free daily spin for bonus coins

For the whole family

  • Simple rules for kids and grandparents, with real strategy
  • English, Gujarati, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic and Japanese
  • Sound, vibration and fast computer moves can each be switched on or off

Free and Pro Pass

Kodi Junction is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Ads never appear during a match, and video ads play only when you choose to watch one for coins. The Heritage Pro Pass, a one-time purchase through Google Play, removes banner and between-match ads and adds 2,000 coins, the Diwali Festival board and a Pro badge. Coin packs are also available.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Your coins, unlocked items and settings are stored on your phone. The internet is used for Google Play purchases and for ads from Google AdMob, which asks for consent where the law requires it. Read the privacy policy and terms.
