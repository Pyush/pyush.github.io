Undercover

One phone. One secret word. One impostor hiding in plain sight. A social deduction party game for 3 to 12 friends.

Scan to installScan with your phone's camera to open Google Play.
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

How to play

  • Pass the phone: each player privately reveals their word
  • Civilians share a word; the Undercover gets a similar one
  • Mr. White gets no word and has to bluff
  • Give one-word clues, discuss and vote someone out

Roles & twists

  • Choose how many Undercovers and Mr. Whites play
  • An eliminated Mr. White can guess the word and steal the win
  • Match recap reveals every role and crowns an MVP
  • Share a victory card with your friends

Words

  • 85 word pairs in 6 categories
  • Casual, Food & Drink, Objects & Tech, Travel & Places
  • Nature & Animals, Pop Culture & Media
  • Create your own custom word pairs

Made for game night

  • 3 to 12 players on a single device
  • Built-in discussion timer, sounds and haptics
  • Win history, role stats and player rankings
  • English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi and Chinese

Free with ads

Undercover is free to play. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and you can watch a short optional ad to unlock custom word packs. There are no in-app purchases.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Player names, custom words and match stats stay on your phone, and gameplay works offline. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; you can change your ad choices in the app's Settings. Read the privacy policy.
