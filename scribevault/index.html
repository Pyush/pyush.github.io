ScribeVault

Speak, and it writes it down. AI transcription that runs on your phone, so your recordings are never uploaded.

Scan to installScan with your phone's camera to open Google Play.
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

On-device transcription

  • Record meetings, lectures and voice memos
  • Speech-to-text runs on your phone, offline after a one-time model download
  • 18 languages, each in its own script, including Hindi in Devanagari
  • Translate speech into English

Speakers, summaries & tasks

  • Speaker detection labels who said what; rename speakers anytime
  • Summary of the key points
  • Action items with deadlines, and Add to Calendar
  • Search every transcript, with highlighted matches

Import, review & export

  • Import audio files, or share a voice message to ScribeVault
  • Tap a sentence to play it; skip silences
  • Export as Markdown, plain text or SRT subtitles
  • PDF export with ScribeVault Pro

Private & secure

  • Recordings and notes never leave your phone
  • AES-256 encrypted notes, with the key in the Android Keystore
  • Optional lock with fingerprint, face or device PIN
  • No account, no ads, no analytics

Free and Pro

ScribeVault is free to download and includes 3 transcriptions a week, up to 2 minutes each. ScribeVault Pro, a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play, removes the weekly limit and the 2-minute cap and adds PDF export. You can cancel anytime in Google Play.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Audio is transcribed on your device and never sent to a server; the internet is used only to download speech models and for Google Play billing. There are no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
