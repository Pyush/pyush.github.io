BrainBoost: Brain Training

Quick daily brain workouts. Twenty short games train your memory, focus, mental math and logic, a fresh Daily Workout picks three challenges for you each day, and simple stats show how you improve.

What's inside

20 brain games

  • Memory: Memory Matrix, Chimp Recall, Card Match, Simon Colors and Dual N-Back
  • Focus: Schulte Grid, Stroop Clash, Go/No-Go, Arrow Flanker, Target Hunt and Focus Shift
  • Math: Speed Math, Equation Challenge, Number Flow and Number Series
  • Logic and words: Pattern Matrix, Outlier Hunt, Spatial Spin, Word Scramble and Brain Rush

Train every day

  • A Daily Workout of three quick challenges picked for you
  • Daily streaks and a Sparks goal to keep the habit going
  • Optional reminders at the time you choose
  • Standard, Practice, Time Attack and Survival modes

See your progress

  • A 75-second Brain Age test you can retake any time
  • Charts and personal bests for every skill
  • Achievements, a Collection Book and a Sparks rewards shop

Google Play Games

  • Cloud save: sign in to keep your progress on a new phone
  • Leaderboards for every game, plus Total XP
  • 23 achievements to unlock
  • Optional: everything works without signing in

Play your way

  • Brain Duel: two players face off on one phone
  • A built-in focus timer for study or work
  • Plays offline, with no account to create
  • More apps: find our other free games and tools
  • In 18 languages, including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish and Portuguese

Free, with an optional upgrade

BrainBoost is free to play, with ads. Ads are a banner on menu screens and an occasional ad between games, never during play. Optional reward videos give bonus Sparks or a second chance. Remove Ads, a one-time purchase through Google Play, turns off the banner and the in-between ads. BrainBoost is for fun and everyday mental practice; it is not a medical or diagnostic tool.

BrainBoost: questions and answers

What games are in BrainBoost?

Twenty games for memory, focus, math, logic and words, including Memory Matrix, Dual N-Back, Schulte Grid, Stroop Clash, Speed Math and Word Scramble, with a Daily Workout of three challenges picked for you.

Does BrainBoost work offline?

Yes. BrainBoost plays offline with no account to create. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional, for cloud save, leaderboards and achievements.

Is the Brain Age test a medical test?

No. BrainBoost is for fun and everyday mental practice. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool.

Is BrainBoost free?

Yes. BrainBoost is free to play, with ads that never appear during play. Remove Ads, a one-time purchase, turns off the banner and the in-between ads.

Privacy

No account to create. Your progress is stored on your phone; if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also saved there so you can restore it on a new device, and your best scores and achievements appear in Play Games. The internet is used for Google Play purchases, for ads from Google AdMob, which asks for consent where the law requires it, and for anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase.
