Compass, spirit level, ruler, expense tracker, HIIT timer & more. Works offline.

Get it on Google Play

What's inside

Compass, level & measurement

  • Compass with Magnetic and True North
  • GPS coordinates, altitude and city name
  • Bubble level, protractor and angle meter
  • On-screen ruler in cm, mm and inches
  • Metal detector and magnifier

Everyday utilities

  • Flashlight with SOS strobe
  • Unit converter
  • Stopwatch with laps and countdown timer
  • Sound meter (decibels)
  • Device and sensor information

MoneyFlow: expenses & budget

  • Income and expenses with categories
  • Monthly budget, envelopes and savings goals
  • Bill reminders and subscriptions
  • Cash counter and optional PIN lock

FocusFlow & workouts

  • Pomodoro focus timer, tasks and habits
  • Study planner, journal and mood tracker
  • Workout log with personal records and streaks
  • HIIT, Tabata and boxing interval timers

Made for India, useful everywhere

Available in English, Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, French, German, Japanese, Nepali, Assamese and Urdu.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Your finances, tasks, journal and workouts are stored only on your device, and every tool works without an internet connection. Hokayantra is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first. Read the privacy policy.
