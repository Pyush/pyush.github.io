Blokonia

A classic falling block puzzle, rebuilt for phones. Rotate and drop the blocks, fill rows to clear them, and chase a new best score as the speed climbs.

Scan to installScan with your phone's camera to open Google Play.
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

Four ways to play

  • Classic: endless levels that speed up, with up to 3 stars per level
  • Daily Challenge: a new puzzle each day, the same pieces for everyone
  • Sprint: clear 40 lines as fast as you can
  • Zen: no game over, just relax and clear lines

Made for phones

  • Big buttons, or tap to rotate, drag to move and flick down to drop
  • Ghost piece shows where each block will land
  • Swap a piece to save it for later
  • Easy or Normal speed, and your game saves automatically

Track your progress

  • Best score, recent games, lines, levels and quads
  • 18 achievements to unlock
  • Share a score card with friends

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, with no account to create
  • Colour-blind patterns and reduced motion in Settings
  • Sound, music and vibration can each be switched off

Free, with optional extras

Blokonia is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Video ads play only when you choose to watch one to continue a game or preview a theme. Remove Ads, a one-time purchase through Google Play, turns off banner and between-game ads. The Neon Pulse and Classic Retro themes, and a Premium Bundle with all three, are also available.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Your scores, stats and settings are stored on your phone. The internet is used for Google Play purchases and for ads from Google AdMob, which asks for consent where the law requires it. Read the privacy policy and terms.
