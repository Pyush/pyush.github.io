Rasidly

Scan a receipt and Rasidly reads the shop, date, total and tax, with no internet and no account. GST, bills, warranties and documents in one private vault.

Scan to installScan with your phone's camera to open Google Play.
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

Scan in seconds

  • On-device text recognition reads the shop, date, total and tax
  • Scan one receipt or a whole stack at once
  • Share photos and PDF invoices in from email, Files or WhatsApp
  • Spots duplicates before you save them twice

GST and tax time

  • GST, VAT or sales tax on every receipt
  • GST input tax credit report with CGST, SGST, IGST and cess
  • Checks supplier GSTINs and flags invoices to look at again
  • Reports by tax year or quarter, with PDF and CSV exports

Bills, warranties & documents

  • Bills to pay, including repeating ones, with reminders
  • Return-window reminders before the last day to return
  • Warranty tracker with one-tap claims
  • Insurance, PUC, licence and passport, reminded before they expire

Money insights

  • Monthly spending by category and shop
  • Budgets with alerts as you near your limit
  • Reimbursements for your employer or clients, and a mileage log
  • English, Hindi, Spanish, French and German, with any currency

Free and Pro

Rasidly is free to download and includes 10 scans a month, bills and reminders, insights, one budget, the GST report, one warranty and two documents. Rasidly Pro, a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play, adds unlimited scans, PDF and CSV exports, claim PDFs, the mileage log, custom categories, and unlimited warranties, documents and budgets. You can cancel anytime in Google Play.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Receipts are read and stored on your phone and never uploaded; the internet is used only for Google Play billing and for usage metrics that Google ML Kit sends about text recognition. There are no ads. Rasidly keeps records and does not give tax advice. Read the privacy policy and terms of use.
