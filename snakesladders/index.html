Snakes & Ladders: Editions

The classic dice board game, beautifully animated. Roll the dice, climb the ladders, dodge the snakes and race to the top, in five hand-made editions.

Scan to installScan with your phone's camera to open Google Play.
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

Five editions

  • Classic: wooden ladders and lively animated snakes on 100 squares
  • High Contrast: bold black, white and yellow, with shaped tokens
  • Moksha Patam: virtues and vices, inspired by the ancient Indian game
  • Serpientes y Escaleras: scattered numbers and star checkpoint squares
  • Winding Path: a quicker 65-square garden trail

Play your way

  • Play vs Computer: you against one to three computer players
  • Pass and Play: 2 to 4 players on one phone or tablet
  • Classic rules by default
  • Optional house rules: need a 6 to start, bounce back, two dice, bumping or an upside-down board

Easy to see, easy to play

  • Full TalkBack support: the board is described and every roll is read out
  • Token shapes, arrows on snakes and ladders, and reduced motion
  • Large text supported, and a turn log of every move

Play anywhere

  • Works offline, with no account to create
  • Each edition has its own board, art and sounds
  • Sound and vibration can each be switched off

Free, with optional extras

Snakes & Ladders: Editions is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Classic and High Contrast are free. Moksha Patam, Serpientes y Escaleras and Winding Path are one-time edition packs, and you can watch a short video to play a full demo game of any pack first. There are never ads during play: a banner on the menu and an occasional ad between games, which Remove ads, a one-time purchase through Google Play, turns off.

Snakes & Ladders: questions and answers

Which editions are included?

Classic and High Contrast are free. Moksha Patam, Serpientes y Escaleras and Winding Path are one-time edition packs, and you can watch a short video to play a full demo game of any pack first.

How many players can play?

Play against one to three computer players, or pass and play with 2 to 4 players on one phone or tablet.

Does Snakes & Ladders: Editions work offline?

Yes. It works offline with no account to create.

Is it accessible?

Yes. It has full TalkBack support, so the board is described and every roll is read out, plus token shapes, arrows on snakes and ladders, reduced motion and large text.

Privacy

No sign-in and no accounts. Your games and settings are stored on your phone. The internet is used for Google Play purchases and for ads from Google AdMob, which asks for consent where the law requires it. Read the privacy policy and terms.
